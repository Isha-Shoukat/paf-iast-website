Who We Are

A Vision for Technical Excellence

Pak-Austria Fachhochschule: Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology (PAF-IAST) is Pakistan's first foreign-collaborative engineering university, located in Mang, Haripur, KPK.

Established as an initiative of the KPK Government in collaboration with Austrian and Chinese universities, PAF-IAST focuses on applied education, skill training, and human resource development.

Our Story

PAF-IAST Journey

From vision to Pakistan's premier applied sciences university — a milestone-filled journey since 2019.

2019

University Founded

PAF-IAST established by KPK Government as Pakistan's first foreign-collaborative engineering university in Mang, Haripur.

2020

First Batch Enrolled

First cohort of students began their academic journey. Programs in AI, Nanotechnology, and Agriculture launched.

2021

Research Centers Established

Sino-Pak Center for AI inaugurated. Business Incubation Center (BIC) and ORIC started operations.

2022

Railway Engineering Launched

Pakistan's only Railway Engineering program launched. International partnerships with Austrian and German universities formalized.

2023

Special Technology Zone

STZ approval granted. $10M+ innovation fund established. HPC Data Center operational for AI research.

2024–25

4,206+ Students Strong

University reaches 4,206+ enrolled students, 70+ PhD faculty, 132 international learners, and 484 proud alumni.

Your Journey Starts Here

Admissions at PAF-IAST are merit-based. Applications are submitted online through the official portal.

Eligibility Criteria

Engineering Programs: FSc Pre-Engineering with minimum 60% marks
Science Programs: FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS with 50% marks
Mandatory Internships: Industry internship required every semester
Entry Test: NTS / University own entry test required
